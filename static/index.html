    How to Search

    ETSI Portal

    • Standard Number: EN 301 489-12, EN 300 328, etc.
    • Keywords: EMC, radio equipment, bluetooth, etc.
    • Technology: 5G, WiFi, LTE, etc.

    Direct search: Opens ETSI portal with automatic search.

    CEN-CENELEC Portal

    • Standard Number: 18031-1:2024, 50360:2017, etc.
    • Keywords: Safety, EMC, machinery, etc.
    • Product Type: Medical devices, machinery, etc.

    Manual search: Standard number copied to clipboard for pasting.
